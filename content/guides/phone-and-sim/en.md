title: Getting a phone and SIM
description: How to get a phone contract or SIM card in Japan: what documents you need, how to choose a plan, and rules to follow so you do not get into trouble.
---
## What do I need to get a phone contract?
1. Your residence card.
2. A payment method: a bank account or a credit card in your name.
3. Some shops ask for proof of address, such as a utility bill.

## How do I choose a phone plan?
1. Big carriers have shops and staff support. Low-cost SIM and online plans are often much cheaper.
2. If your phone is unlocked, you can use an eSIM or a SIM card from another company.
3. Check the monthly price after discounts end, the contract period, and the cancellation fee.

## What should I be careful about?
1. Never give your SIM or phone contract to another person. Lending or selling a phone line used for crime can get you arrested.
2. Before you leave Japan, cancel your contract and pay the last bill.
3. Unpaid phone bills can make it hard to get a phone or loan in Japan later.
