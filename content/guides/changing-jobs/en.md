title: Can I change jobs? Rules by status of residence
description: Whether you can change jobs in Japan depends on your status of residence. Rules for Specified Skilled Workers, technical intern trainees, students and other visas, the 14-day notice, and free phone help.
---
Whether you can change jobs depends on your status of residence (visa). This page explains the general rules. For your own case, ask immigration or a gyoseishoshi (licensed administrative scrivener).

## Can I change jobs as a Specified Skilled Worker?
1. Yes. But you must get permission to change your status of residence before you start the new job.
2. You need this even in the same field. Your permission names your company.
3. You cannot work at the new company while immigration checks your application.
4. Apply at the immigration office or online. A fee is needed.
5. Unpaid taxes or social insurance can slow down or stop the permission.

## What must I tell immigration when I leave or join a company?
1. Many work visas must send a notice within 14 days after the contract ends. Send another one within 14 days after a new contract.
2. This includes Specified Skilled Worker, Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, Nursing Care and Skilled Labor.
3. You can send it online, at the immigration office, or by mail.

## Can a technical intern trainee change jobs?
1. In principle, no. You can only do the work at your training company. Part-time jobs are not allowed.
2. If training becomes very hard, and you want to continue, the Organization for Technical Intern Training (OTIT) can help you move to a new company. For example, if the company closes or your rights are violated.
3. Do not run away from your workplace. Working somewhere else is illegal. You can be deported.
4. OTIT free phone in your language: Vietnamese 0120-250-168 (Monday to Saturday), Indonesian 0120-250-192 (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday).
5. Hours: weekdays 11:00 to 19:00, Saturday and Sunday 9:00 to 17:00.
6. OTIT Toyama office: 0120-163-423, weekdays 9:00 to 17:00.

## What changes in April 2027?
1. From April 1, 2027, technical intern training will change to a new system, "Employment for Skill Development" (Ikusei Shuro).
2. In the new system, a worker can change companies by their own choice after 1 to 2 years. The period depends on the field.
3. There are conditions, such as a skill test and a Japanese test. The new job must be the same type of work.
4. Some details are still being decided. Check the official Q&A.

## Can I work full-time after school as a student?
1. While you study, you can only work part-time with permission. The limit is 28 hours a week, or 8 hours a day in long school holidays.
2. To work full-time after school, you must change your status of residence. For example, to Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, or Specified Skilled Worker.
3. See our page [For international students](/en/students/).

## Are there visas with no job limits?
1. Permanent Resident, Spouse or Child of Japanese National, Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident, and Long-Term Resident have no limit on the type of work.

## What should I check before I apply for a job on this site?
1. "Foreign nationals welcome" does not mean your visa allows that job.
2. Check that your status of residence allows the work. Ask the company and immigration.
3. This site only shows job listings. We do not introduce workers or handle applications.
