title: Driving in snow
description: How to drive safely in Toyama's winter: when to change to winter tires, what to keep in your car, how to drive on snowy roads and where to check road conditions.
---
Toyama gets heavy snow, mostly from December to February. Snow can come suddenly and roads can close. If you drive, prepare in November.

## How do I prepare my car before the snow?
1. Change to winter tires (studless tires). Most people in Toyama change in November. Normal tires on snow are very dangerous.
2. Put a snow brush, a steel shovel, gloves and a blanket in your car.
3. Check your windshield washer fluid. Use a winter type that does not freeze.

## How should I drive on snowy days?
1. Leave 15 to 30 minutes earlier. Clear all snow from your car, including the roof and lights, before you drive.
2. Drive slowly and keep a long distance from the car in front. Brake early and gently.
3. Be careful on bridges, slopes, curves, crossings and in tunnels. Bridges and shaded roads freeze first.
4. Many roads in Toyama have snow-melting pipes that spray water. The road is wet and can be slippery. Slow down.
5. Watch for snow falling from roofs, bridges and road signs.
6. Do not park on the road. It blocks the snow plows.
7. When heavy snow is forecast, do not go out by car unless you must. Tell your workplace early if you cannot come.

## Where can I check road conditions?
1. The Toyama Prefecture road information site shows road cameras and snow depth.
2. Road traffic information in Japanese: 050-3369-6616 (JARTIC Toyama).
