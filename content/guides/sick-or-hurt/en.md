title: If you get sick or hurt
description: What to do if you get sick or injured in Toyama: calling an ambulance, choosing a clinic, what to bring, night and holiday clinics, and hospitals that speak your language.
---
## What do I do in an emergency?
1. Call 119 for an ambulance. Say "kyukyu desu" (it is an emergency), then your address slowly.
2. Ambulances are free in Japan. Use them only for serious cases.

## Where do I go if it is not an emergency?
1. In Japan, people usually go to a small clinic first, not a big hospital. Big hospitals may charge an extra fee without a referral letter.
2. Choose the right clinic: naika (internal medicine, for colds and fever), geka or seikei-geka (injuries, bones), shika (dentist), jibika (ear, nose and throat), hifuka (skin), fujinka (women).
3. Bring your residence card, health insurance card (or My Number card) and cash. Many clinics do not take credit cards.
4. With health insurance, you usually pay 30% of the cost.
5. At night and on holidays, go to a holiday or night emergency clinic (kyujitsu yakan kyukan center). Call first if you can.
6. For a sick child at night, call #8000 (in Japanese) for advice.

## How can I find a hospital that speaks my language?
1. The national Medical Information Net lets you search hospitals and pharmacies by language and by night or holiday service.
2. If you need an interpreter, the Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center can help by phone on weekdays (076-441-6330).
3. Medicine: show your prescription at a pharmacy. Tell the pharmacist about allergies and other medicine you take.
