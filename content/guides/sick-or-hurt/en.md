title: If you get sick or hurt
description: What to do if you get sick or injured in Toyama: calling an ambulance, choosing a clinic, what to bring, night and holiday clinics in Toyama City and Takaoka with hours, and the children's medical phone line #8000.
---
## What do I do in an emergency?
1. Call 119 for an ambulance. Say "kyukyu desu" (it is an emergency), then your address slowly.
2. Ambulances are free in Japan. Use them only for serious cases, for example trouble breathing, chest pain, a bad injury or if someone does not wake up.

## Where do I go if it is not an emergency?
1. In Japan, people usually go to a small clinic first, not a big hospital. Big hospitals may charge an extra fee (often several thousand yen) without a referral letter.
2. Choose the right clinic: naika (internal medicine, for colds and fever), geka or seikei-geka (injuries, bones), shika (dentist), jibika (ear, nose and throat), hifuka (skin), fujinka (women), shonika (children).
3. Many clinics are closed on Sundays, holidays and one weekday afternoon. Check the hours before you go.
4. Bring your residence card, your health insurance card or My Number card, and cash. Many clinics do not take credit cards.
5. With health insurance, you usually pay 30% of the cost. Without it, you pay 100%.

## Where can I go at night or on holidays in Toyama City?
1. Toyama City Medical Association Emergency Center (Toyama-shi Ishikai Kyukan Center): 2-76 Imaizumi-hokubu-machi, Toyama City, next to Toyama City Hospital. Phone 076-425-9999.
2. Internal medicine, children and surgery: Monday to Friday 19:00 to 24:00. Saturday 14:00 to 24:00 (children 19:00 to 24:00). Sundays and holidays 9:00 to 17:30 and 18:30 to 24:00.
3. Eye, ear-nose-throat and skin doctors are there on some Sundays only.
4. Reception starts 20 minutes before and ends 15 minutes before closing. Usually you get medicine for one day only, so go to a normal clinic the next day.
5. If you forget your insurance card, you pay the full cost first.

## Where can I go at night or on holidays in Takaoka?
1. Takaoka City Emergency Medical Center (Takaoka-shi Kyukan Iryo Center): 7-1 Honmaru-machi, Takaoka. Near the Kyukan Iryo Center-mae stop of the Manyosen tram. Phone 0766-25-7111 (this number does not give medical advice).
2. Internal medicine, children and surgery. Monday to Friday 19:30 to 22:30. Saturday 19:00 to 22:30. Sundays and holidays 9:00 to 17:00 and 18:00 to 22:30.
3. After 22:30, the hospital on duty that day takes patients.
4. In other areas, ask your city office or check the national Medical Information Net.

## Who can I call for advice about a sick child at night?
1. Children's medical phone line: #8000 or 076-444-1099 (in Japanese). A nurse gives advice, and a children's doctor if needed.
2. Weekdays 19:00 to 9:00 the next morning. Saturday 13:00 to 9:00. Sundays and holidays 9:00 to 9:00.
3. They cannot diagnose by phone. If the child is very sick, call 119.

## How can I find a hospital that speaks my language?
1. The national Medical Information Net lets you search hospitals and pharmacies by language and by night or holiday service.
2. If you need an interpreter, the Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center can help by phone on weekdays (076-441-6330), including three-way calls with the hospital.
3. Medicine: show your prescription at a pharmacy. Tell the pharmacist about allergies and other medicine you take.
