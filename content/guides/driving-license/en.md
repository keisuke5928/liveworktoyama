title: Driving in Japan: license and rules
description: Which driver's license you need to drive in Toyama, how to change a foreign license to a Japanese one, and the main traffic rules and penalties.
---
In Toyama, many jobs and daily errands need a car. Driving without a valid license is a crime, so check your license first.

## Which license can I drive with?
1. A Japanese driver's license.
2. An international driving permit in the official format of the 1949 Geneva Convention.
3. A license from Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Monaco or Taiwan, with an official Japanese translation (for example from your embassy).
4. Options 2 and 3 can be used for a maximum of one year.
5. You also need a license to ride a moped (gentsuki).

## How do I get a Japanese license?
1. Change your foreign license to a Japanese one (gaimen kirikae). You apply at the driver's license center of the prefectural police.
2. You must have lived in the country that issued your license for at least 3 months after you got it.
3. If your knowledge and driving skills are judged to be enough, you do not need to take the knowledge and skills tests.
4. Or take the test in Japan. If you finish a course at a designated driving school, you do not need to take the skills test.
5. Ask the driver's license center which documents to bring.

## What are the main rules on the road?
1. Drive on the left.
2. Never drink and drive. It is also illegal to lend your car to a person who has been drinking, or to ask a person who has been drinking to drive.
3. Everyone in the car must wear a seat belt. Children under 6 must sit in a child seat.
4. Do not use your phone while driving.
5. Wear a helmet on a motorcycle.
6. Show your license if a police officer asks.

## What else should I know?
1. A Japanese license has an expiry date. A postcard comes before renewal time. You cannot drive with an expired license.
2. When your address or name changes, tell the police station.
3. Traffic violations give you penalty points. Too many points in three years can lead to suspension or cancellation of your license.
4. In winter, read our guide on driving in snow.
