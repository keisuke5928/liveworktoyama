title: City office and immigration notices after moving
description: What to do at the city office after you move to Toyama (address registration within 14 days, My Number, insurance) and which changes you must report to immigration.
---
## What do I need to do at the city office after I move?
1. Register your address at the city office within 14 days after you move in. Bring your residence card. Your new address is written on the back.
2. When you move to another city, report your move-out at the old city office and your move-in at the new one.
3. You can apply for a My Number card. It is used for taxes, insurance and many online services.
4. If your company does not give you health insurance and pension, join National Health Insurance and the National Pension at the city office.
5. Ask for the garbage calendar and information for foreign residents.

## What do I need to report to the Immigration Services Agency?
1. If you change or leave your company or school, many visa types must report this to immigration within 14 days. You can do it online.
2. Your residence card has an expiry date. Apply to extend your period of stay before it ends.
3. For questions about your own visa, ask immigration or a gyoseishoshi.
