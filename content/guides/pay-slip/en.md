title: Reading your pay slip
description: How to read a Japanese pay slip (kyuyo meisaisho): basic pay, overtime, insurance, pension, income tax and resident tax, and what to check every month.
---
Your pay slip (kyuyo meisaisho) shows what you earned and what was taken out. Keep every pay slip.

## What did I earn (shikyu)?
1. Kihonkyu: basic pay.
2. Zangyo teate: overtime pay. Overtime is paid at least 25% more than normal pay.
3. Other allowances: commuting, housing, night work and more.

## What was taken out (kojo)?
1. Kenko hoken: health insurance.
2. Kosei nenkin: pension.
3. Koyo hoken: employment insurance.
4. Shotokuzei: income tax.
5. Juminzei: resident tax. It usually starts from June of your second year in Japan, so take-home pay can go down.
6. Rent, dormitory fees or meals may also be taken out if you agreed to it.

## What should I check every month?
1. Compare your working hours and overtime with your own record.
2. Check that your pay is not below the minimum wage in Toyama.
3. If something looks wrong, ask your company first. If it is not solved, call a labor consultation line.
