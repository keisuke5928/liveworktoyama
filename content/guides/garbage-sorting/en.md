title: Garbage sorting
description: How to sort and put out garbage in Toyama: where to get your city's garbage calendar, common garbage types, collection rules and large items.
---
Garbage rules are one of the biggest causes of trouble with neighbors. Every city in Toyama has its own rules, bags and calendar.

## What should I do first?
1. Get the garbage calendar and sorting guide from your city office, your apartment manager or your company. Many cities have versions in English and other languages.
2. Find your collection point. Ask your neighbors or manager.
3. Some cities require special garbage bags sold at supermarkets and convenience stores. Check which bag your city uses.

## What are the common types of garbage?
1. Burnable garbage: food waste, paper scraps, and in many cities small plastic items. Drain water from food waste.
2. Plastic containers and packaging: rinse them lightly.
3. Cans, glass bottles and PET bottles: rinse them. Remove caps and labels from PET bottles.
4. Large items such as furniture or bicycles: you need to book a collection and pay a fee.
5. Air conditioners, TVs, fridges and washing machines cannot go out as normal garbage. Ask the shop or your city.

## What rules should I keep?
1. Put garbage out in the morning of the collection day, usually by 8:30. Not the night before.
2. If your garbage is left with a sticker, it was sorted wrongly. Take it back, sort it again and put it out on the right day.
3. When you move out, do not leave garbage or furniture behind.
