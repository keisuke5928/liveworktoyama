title: Garbage sorting
description: How to sort and put out garbage in Toyama: rules in Toyama City and Takaoka (bags, times, guides in your language), common garbage types, large items and what to do when you move.
---
Garbage rules are one of the biggest causes of trouble with neighbors. Every city in Toyama has its own rules, bags and calendar.

## What should I do first?
1. Get the garbage calendar and sorting guide from your city office, your apartment manager or your company.
2. Find your collection point. In Takaoka, the local neighborhood association or the apartment manager looks after it. Ask your neighbors or manager.
3. Check which bag your city uses. Some cities require special garbage bags sold at supermarkets and drugstores.

## What are the rules in Toyama City?
1. Put garbage out by 8:00 in the morning on the collection day, at your collection point.
2. Burnable garbage is collected twice a week. Use a strong clear or semi-clear bag and tie it tightly.
3. Drain water from kitchen waste. Cut branches, carpets and wooden furniture to 1 meter or less and tie them.
4. Plastic items longer than 50 cm and up to 1 meter can go out as burnable garbage.
5. A guide "How to sort and put out household garbage" is available in English, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese and Indonesian. You can also get posters at the Citizens Section on the 1st floor of City Hall. The collection calendar itself is in Japanese.
6. Toyama City has an online "garbage sorting dictionary" where you can search each item.

## What are the rules in Takaoka?
1. Burnable garbage must go in Takaoka's designated garbage bags (10, 20 and 45 liters), sold at supermarkets and drugstores.
2. Put it out by 8:30 in the morning on the collection day (this can be different in some areas).
3. Garbage in the wrong bag or not sorted gets a warning sticker and is left behind.
4. A guide for foreign residents is available in English, Chinese, Portuguese and Vietnamese.
5. A new calendar is given out every March. Collection may skip national holidays.

## What are the common types of garbage?
1. Burnable garbage: food waste, paper scraps, and in many cities small plastic items. Drain water from food waste.
2. Plastic containers and packaging: rinse them lightly.
3. Cans, glass bottles and PET bottles: rinse them. Remove caps and labels from PET bottles.
4. Large items such as furniture or bicycles: you usually need to book a collection and pay a fee, or take them to a facility. Ask your city.
5. Air conditioners, TVs, fridges and washing machines cannot go out as normal garbage. Ask the shop or your city.
6. Batteries, spray cans and lighters have special rules. Never put them in burnable garbage.

## What should I do when I move?
1. Ask your city how to put out a lot of garbage at once. In Takaoka you can ask the Environmental Policy Division (0766-22-2144) about a collection by appointment.
2. Do not leave garbage or furniture in your room or at the collection point.
