title: Earthquakes and heavy snow
description: How to prepare for earthquakes, tsunami and heavy snow in Toyama: the free Safety tips app, hazard maps, emergency supplies and what to do when it happens.
---
In January 2024, the Noto Peninsula earthquake also caused damage in Toyama. Prepare before it happens.

## How can I prepare now?
1. Install the free Safety tips app. It sends earthquake, tsunami and weather warnings in 15 languages.
2. Check your city hazard map and your nearest evacuation shelter. Walk there once.
3. Keep water, food, a flashlight, a phone battery and your medicine for at least 3 days.
4. Fix tall furniture to the wall. Do not sleep next to tall shelves.
5. Decide with your family or friends where to meet. Know your embassy contact.

## What do I do when an earthquake happens?
1. Get under a table and protect your head until the shaking stops.
2. Near the sea, if there is a strong quake or a tsunami warning, move to high ground right away. Do not wait.
3. Turn off gas and heaters when it is safe. Watch for broken glass.
4. Get information from the app, your city and NHK.

## How do I stay safe in heavy snow?
1. Before you start your car, clear snow around the exhaust pipe. Blocked exhaust can cause carbon monoxide poisoning.
2. If you are stuck in snow in your car, keep the exhaust clear and open a window a little.
3. Do not clear snow from roofs alone. Many accidents happen every winter.
