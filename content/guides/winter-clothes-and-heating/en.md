title: Winter clothes and heating
description: What to wear in a Toyama winter and how to heat your room safely: winter boots, layers, kerosene heaters, frozen pipes and mold.
---
Toyama winters are not as cold as Hokkaido, but they are wet, snowy and dark early. Good shoes and a warm room make a big difference.

## What clothes do I need for winter in Toyama?
1. Buy waterproof winter boots with good grip. Snow and slush make normal shoes wet and slippery. Home centers and shoe shops sell them from November.
2. Wear layers: thermal underwear, a sweater and a waterproof jacket. Shops and trains are warm, outside is cold.
3. An umbrella is useful all winter. Toyama has many rainy and snowy days.

## How do I use a heater safely?
1. Kerosene heaters are common. Gas stations and some home centers sell kerosene. Some shops deliver it.
2. Open a window for a few minutes every hour to let fresh air in.
3. Never use a gas or kerosene heater while sleeping. Turn it off before you go out.
4. Keep heaters away from curtains, clothes and futons.

## What problems can happen at home in winter?
1. On very cold nights, water pipes can freeze. Ask your landlord or company what to do.
2. Condensation on windows can cause mold. Wipe windows and air your room.
3. Do not climb on the roof to clear snow alone. Ask your landlord or company.

## How do I stop water pipes from freezing?
1. When the forecast says minus 4 degrees or colder, leave a tap running with a very thin stream of water overnight.
2. Wrap pipes that are outside or in a cold place with cloth or insulation.
3. If a pipe is frozen, do not pour boiling water on it. It can break. Warm it slowly with lukewarm water or wait.
4. If water leaks, close the main valve and call your landlord, company or the city water office.
