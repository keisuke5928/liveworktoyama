title: Opening a bank account
description: How to open a bank account in Toyama as a foreign resident: what to bring, which banks are common, how long it takes, and what you must never do with your account.
---
You need a bank account to receive your salary and pay rent, phone and utility bills.

## What do I need to open a bank account?
1. Your residence card. Your address must be registered at the city office first.
2. A phone number in Japan.
3. Your hanko (personal seal) if you have one. Some banks accept a signature.
4. Some banks ask for your employee ID, a letter from your company or school, or how long you have lived in Japan.

## Which bank should I choose?
1. Ask your workplace which bank they use for salaries. It can make transfers easier.
2. The Japan Post Bank (yucho) and local banks such as Hokuriku Bank have many branches in Toyama.
3. Opening can take one to two weeks. Your cash card arrives by mail, so make sure your name is on your mailbox.
4. Set up a smartphone app to check your balance.

## What must I never do with my bank account?
1. Never sell, lend or give your bank account, cash card or PIN to anyone, even when you leave Japan. It is a crime in Japan and your account can be used for fraud.
2. When you leave Japan, close your accounts at the bank.
