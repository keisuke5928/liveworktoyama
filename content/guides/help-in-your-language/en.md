title: Where to get help in your language
description: Free help for foreign residents in Toyama: emergency numbers, the Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center (phone, hours, 22 languages) and phone lines for problems at work with numbers for each language.
---
You are not alone. These services are free or low cost, and many of them support your language.

## What number do I call in an emergency?
1. Fire or ambulance: call 119.
2. Police: call 110.
3. Say your address slowly. If you cannot speak Japanese well, say "English, please" and stay on the line.
4. If you do not know your address, tell them a big building, shop or crossing you can see.

## Where can I get help with problems in daily life?
1. The Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center (Toyama Gaikokujin One-Stop Soudan Center) gives free advice about daily life. Your personal information is kept secret.
2. Phone: 076-441-6330, 076-441-5654 or 080-5852-2234.
3. Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 (closed on national holidays).
4. Place: Toyama International Center, Intec Building 4F, 5-5 Ushijimashin-machi, Toyama City. It is near the north exit of Toyama Station.
5. Staff speak Japanese, English, Chinese, Portuguese, Korean, Russian, Telugu, Hindi and Vietnamese.
6. Interpreting by tablet or phone is available in 22 languages, including Indonesian, Tagalog, Nepali, Myanmar, Thai and Khmer. Hours can be different for each language, so please call first.
7. A three-way call is possible: you, the center and a school, city office or hospital can talk at the same time.
8. You can ask about housing, money, school, hospitals, Japanese classes, problems with neighbors and more. There is a private room for consultations.

## Who can I talk to about problems at work?
1. Unpaid wages, sudden dismissal, very long hours: first talk to your company or your support organization.
2. Phone line for foreign workers (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare), 10:00 to 15:00 except 12:00 to 13:00. Calls are charged.
3. English 0570-001-701 and Vietnamese 0570-001-706: Monday to Friday.
4. Indonesian 0570-001-715: Tuesday and Thursday.
5. Other languages: Chinese, Portuguese, Spanish, Tagalog, Myanmar, Nepali, Korean, Thai, Khmer and Mongolian. See the leaflet below for numbers and days.
6. Evening and weekend line (free call): weekdays 17:00 to 22:00, weekends and holidays 9:00 to 21:00. English 0120-531-401 (every day), Vietnamese 0120-531-406 (Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday to Sunday), Indonesian 0120-613-803 (check the leaflet for days).
7. You can also visit the Labour Standards Inspection Office for your workplace.
