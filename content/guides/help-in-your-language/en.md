title: Where to get help in your language
description: Free help for foreign residents in Toyama: emergency numbers, the Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center (22 languages) and phone lines for problems at work.
---
You are not alone. These services are free or low cost, and many of them support your language.

## What number do I call in an emergency?
1. Fire or ambulance: call 119.
2. Police: call 110.
3. Say your address slowly. If you cannot speak Japanese well, say "English, please" and stay on the line.

## Where can I get help with problems in daily life?
1. Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center: free consultation about daily life in 22 languages and easy Japanese, with interpreting by phone. It keeps your secrets.
2. Hours: weekdays 9:00 to 17:00 (closed on holidays). Phone: 076-441-6330.
3. Place: Intec Building 4F, 5-5 Ushijimashin-machi, Toyama City.
4. You can ask about housing, money, school, hospitals, Japanese classes, problems with neighbors and more.

## Who can I talk to about problems at work?
1. Unpaid wages, sudden dismissal, very long hours: first talk to your company or your support organization.
2. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare has a phone line for foreign workers in 13 languages, and a free evening and weekend line. Check the leaflet below for numbers and days for your language.
3. You can also visit the Labour Standards Inspection Office for your workplace.
