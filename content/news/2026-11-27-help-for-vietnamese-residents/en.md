+++ body
On October 23, 2026, the Immigration Services Agency of Japan published a guide for Vietnamese people in trouble. It was made with the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Organization for Technical Intern Training (OTIT) and the Embassy of Vietnam.

For technical intern trainees: if you face violence, harassment or serious law or contract violations at your workplace, you may be able to change your training company. Talk to OTIT in Vietnamese. People who stopped training, including people who left their workplace, can also talk to OTIT about restarting or going home.

OTIT Vietnamese line (free): 0120-250-168. Monday to Friday 11:00 to 19:00, Saturday 9:00 to 17:00. You can also send an email at any time from the QR code on the leaflet.

For former trainees who stayed past their visa: the guide explains the departure order system. If your only violation is overstay and you go to immigration yourself before you are caught, you may be able to go home without being detained. In that case, the period you cannot enter Japan becomes 1 year instead of 5 years. There are other conditions too.

The Embassy of Vietnam also helps with passports for going home. You can apply online for a temporary passport, and bring the documents to the embassy or a consulate.
+++ meaning
1. If you have problems at work, talk early. You can use the free Vietnamese line, or the Toyama Foreign Resident Support Center (076-441-6330).
2. Do not leave your workplace without telling anyone. Working somewhere else without the right status is illegal.
3. We do not give advice about your own case. For your visa, ask immigration or a gyoseishoshi (licensed administrative scrivener).
