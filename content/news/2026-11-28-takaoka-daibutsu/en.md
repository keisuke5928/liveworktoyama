+++ short
1. The Takaoka Daibutsu is a bronze seated Buddha, about 16 meters tall, at Daibutsu-ji temple in Takaoka.
2. It is about 10 minutes on foot from the north exit of Takaoka Station. You do not need a car.
3. There is no fixed fee. You give a donation of any amount (shino).
+++ body
## At a glance
1. Cost: donation of any amount (shino).
2. Gallery under the statue: 9:00 to 17:00. The temple has some irregular closing days.
3. Access: about 10 minutes on foot from the north exit (Kojo Koen exit) of Takaoka Station on the Ainokaze Toyama Railway. Or about 3 minutes on foot from the Sakashita-machi stop of the Manyo Line tram.
4. From Toyama Station: take the Ainokaze Toyama Railway to Takaoka Station. Check the time and fare on the railway's website.
5. Address: 11-29 Otemachi, Takaoka. Phone (Japanese): 0766-23-9156, 9:00 to 17:00.

## What can I see?
The Buddha is made of bronze by Takaoka's metal craftsmen, a local tradition. Work started in 1907 and took 26 years. The Takaoka tourism site says people call it "the most handsome Buddha in Japan".

Inside the base, there is a gallery with Buddhist paintings. You can also see the face of the second Daibutsu, which survived a big fire in 1900.

When we visited in November 2026, the statue was lit up after dark, as in the photo. It is a calm place for an evening walk.

## Is it good for a day off?
Yes. It is near the station and costs little, so it fits a short trip on a day off. It is easy to combine with Takaoka Kojo Park nearby. You can go alone, with friends or with family.

## What should I be careful about?
1. It is a temple. Speak quietly and do not climb on anything.
2. In winter, paths can be icy after snow. Wear good shoes and walk slowly.
3. Opening days can change. Check the official page before you go.
