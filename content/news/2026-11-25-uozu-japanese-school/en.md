+++ body
A Japanese language school approved by the Ministry of Education is planned to open in Uozu City in April 2027. It will be the first school of this kind in Toyama Prefecture.

The school plans to accept about 100 students, mainly from Asia. It also plans to support students to find jobs in Toyama after they graduate.

We will share more details, such as how to apply, when official information is published.

In October 2025, Uozu City signed a location agreement for this school with ICA Co., Ltd. It uses the former Matsukura nursery school building. At that time, the plan was to open around October 2026 with 80 students. The newer report says April 2027 and about 100 students.
+++ meaning
1. If you want to study Japanese in Japan, Toyama may become an option from April 2027.
2. International students can work part-time with permission, up to 28 hours a week during school terms. See our page for students.
3. Life in Uozu is easier with a plan: trains and buses are not frequent, and winters are snowy.
