+++ body
The Toyama Labour Bureau raised the Toyama Prefecture minimum wage by 57 yen. From October 1, 2026, it is 1,119 yen an hour. Before, it was 1,062 yen.

## Who does it apply to?
The minimum wage applies to almost all workers in Toyama Prefecture. Your nationality does not matter. It also applies to part-time jobs (arubaito), technical intern trainees and Specified Skilled Workers.

## How do I check my pay?
1. Hourly pay: your hourly pay must be 1,119 yen or more.
2. Daily pay: divide your daily pay by your usual working hours in a day. The answer must be 1,119 yen or more.
3. Monthly pay: divide your monthly pay by your average working hours in a month. The answer must be 1,119 yen or more.
4. Overtime pay, night work pay, bonuses, and commuting and family allowances are not counted in this check.

## What if my pay is lower?
Your company must pay at least the minimum wage, even if you agreed to a lower amount. If your pay is lower, first look at your pay slip and your contract. Then you can ask the Labour Standards Inspection Office or a free phone line.
