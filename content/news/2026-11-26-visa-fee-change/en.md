+++ body
The Immigration Services Agency of Japan changed the fees for residence procedures. The new fees apply to applications made on or after October 1, 2026.

The change covers applications for extension of period of stay, change of status of residence, and permanent residence. The fee now depends on the length of the period of stay you are granted.

If you applied by September 30, 2026, you pay the old fee.

The exact amounts are in the fee table on the official page. Please check it before you apply.
+++ meaning
1. If your residence card expires soon, check the new fee and plan for it in your budget.
2. You can usually apply for an extension from about three months before your period of stay ends.
3. We do not give advice about your own visa. If you are not sure which procedure or fee applies to you, ask immigration or a gyoseishoshi (licensed administrative scrivener).
