1. Uchikawa is a fishing town in Shinminato, Imizu City, with fishing boats, old wooden houses and many bridges along a canal.
2. Walking around is free. You do not need a car: take the Manyo Line tram from Takaoka Station.
3. On clear days, you can see the snowy Tateyama mountains from the bridges, as in our photo.
+++ body
## At a glance
1. Cost: free to walk around.
2. Access: from Takaoka Station (Ainokaze Toyama Railway), take the Manyo Line tram. Get off at "Shinmachiguchi" (for Kawa-no-Eki Shinminato) or "Daiichi Inn Shinminato Crossbay-mae" (for Crossbay Shinminato). It is about 10 to 15 minutes on foot from each stop to the canal.
3. Another way: from Kosugi Station, Imizu City's community bus "Shinminato–Kosugi line" (No. 7).
4. Good places to start: Kawa-no-Eki Shinminato, beside the canal, has the sightseeing boat stop, a cafe and local products. Crossbay Shinminato has restaurants and you can leave your bags there.

## What can I see?
Fishing boats line the canal, between old wooden houses and newer buildings. Small bridges cross the water one after another. Imizu City's tourism site says the town still keeps the life and history of a fishing port. In the evening, the canal is beautiful too.

When we visited in November 2026, the snowy Tateyama mountains and the tower of the Shinminato Bridge were clear behind the canal.

## Is it good for a day off?
Yes. It is free, quiet and good for photos. You can walk slowly alone, with friends or with family. If you live in Takaoka or Imizu, it is close.

## What should I be careful about?
1. People live and work here. Do not go onto boats or private land, and speak quietly.
2. Wind from the sea is cold in winter. Wear a warm coat.
3. Some edges of the canal have no fence. Be careful with children.
