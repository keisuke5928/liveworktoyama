+++ short
1. The Kurobe Gorge trolley train runs the whole line (Unazuki to Keyakidaira) until November 30, 2026.
2. From December 1 to 6, it runs a short round trip from Unazuki to Sasadaira only. Booking is by phone.
3. After that, it stops for the winter. You can get to Unazuki Onsen by train from Toyama, without a car.
+++ body
## At a glance
1. Whole line: until Monday, November 30, 2026. Example one-way fare: Unazuki to Kuronagi, adults 830 yen.
2. Extra days: Tuesday, December 1 to Sunday, December 6. Unazuki to Sasadaira round trip, about 30 minutes each way. Adults 2,260 yen round trip (relax car included). Four trips a day.
3. Booking for December 1 to 6: phone only, Kurobe Gorge Railway Customer Center 0765-62-1011 (9:00 to 16:00). Same-day tickets are also available.
4. From Toyama: take the Toyama Chiho Railway to Unazuki-Onsen Station. From there, Unazuki Station of the trolley train is about 5 minutes on foot. Check the train time and fare on the railway's website.
5. Keyakidaira: because of repair work, you can only go to the station and the visitor center. The railway suggests staying about 1 hour.

## What can I see?
The small open trolley train runs deep into the Kurobe Gorge, along a green-blue river between steep mountains. In our photo from November 2026, the red Shin-Yamabiko Bridge crosses the river among autumn trees near Unazuki Onsen.

## Is it good for a day off?
Yes, if you go soon. The whole line ends on November 30. In December the trip is short, so it is good for a half day. Unazuki Onsen town has hot springs and is nice to visit all year.

## What should I be careful about?
1. It is cold in the gorge. In December, there may be snow. Wear warm clothes, gloves and good shoes.
2. The operating days can change because of the weather. Check the official website before you go.
3. Do not walk into closed areas at Keyakidaira.
