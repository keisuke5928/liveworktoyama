+++ short
1. Kansui Park (Fugan Unga Kansui Koen) has free winter lights every evening, 17:00 to 22:00, until February 28, 2027.
2. It is about 9 minutes on foot from the north exit of Toyama Station. You do not need a car.
3. From December 4, a special design about Toyama Bay lights up the Tenmon Bridge area.
+++ body
## At a glance
1. Cost: free.
2. Dates: until Sunday, February 28, 2027. Every day.
3. Time: 17:00 to 22:00.
4. Access: about 9 minutes on foot from Toyama Station (north exit).
5. Note: from December 1 to 3, the lights in the Tenmon Bridge area are off for work.

## What can I see?
LED lights shine around the park at night. The Tenmon Bridge, the symbol of the park, is lit up. In the fountain and waterfall square, white trees shine in a round pool, with a "waterfall of light" around them.

From December 4, students of the University of Toyama show a special design called "Wrapped in Toyama Bay" at the Tenmon Bridge area. It shows the rich sea of Toyama Bay with many kinds of fish, and a wish for recovery after the Noto Peninsula Earthquake.

## Is it good for a day off?
Yes. It is free and close to the station, so it is easy after work or on a day off. You can go alone, with friends or with family. It is a nice place for photos.

## What should I be careful about?
1. It is cold at night in winter. Wear a warm coat, gloves and good shoes.
2. After snow, paths can be icy. Walk slowly with small steps.
3. Check the official page before you go, because plans can change.
