title: Coming from Indonesia? A TB certificate will be needed from January 4, 2027
description: Japan is starting pre-entry tuberculosis screening for people from Indonesia. From January 4, 2027 (planned), a certificate showing you do not have TB is needed when applying for a COE or visa.

+++ short
1. Japan is starting pre-entry tuberculosis (TB) screening in Indonesia.
2. Checks at designated clinics in Indonesia were planned to start on November 2, 2026.
3. From January 4, 2027 (planned), a "certificate of not having TB" is needed to apply for a COE or visa.
+++ body
Japan checks some people for tuberculosis (TB) before they come to live in Japan for a medium or long period. This already applies to people from the Philippines, Nepal and Vietnam. Indonesia is now being added.

The check is done at a clinic in Indonesia that the Japanese government has designated. A doctor examines you and takes a chest X-ray. If the doctor thinks it is needed, there is also a sputum test. If you do not have TB, you get a "certificate of not having TB" (kekkaku hi-hatsubyo shomeisho).

According to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, designated clinics were planned to start the checks on November 2, 2026. From January 4, 2027 (planned), the certificate must be submitted when you apply for a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) or a visa. The Immigration Services Agency says a copy or scan of the certificate is accepted.

The list of designated clinics is updated as more clinics are ready. Please check the latest list before you book.
+++ meaning
1. If you, your family or a new coworker plans to come from Indonesia, add time for the TB check to the schedule.
2. Employers and schools that bring people from Indonesia should check which applications need the certificate and when.
3. Rules can differ by status of residence. Ask the employer or school, immigration, or a gyoseishoshi (licensed administrative scrivener) about your own case.
